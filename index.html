Biomimicry is the practice of learning from and applying nature's time-tested strategies for a more sustainable and resilient future. From no-clog filters that separate microplastics from water to social media content algorithms — nature's wisdom can be applied everywhere. And sometimes, the solution is even nature itself.

The philosophy

Design informed
by 3.8 billion years
of research

Biomimicry is an approach to innovation that is a return to what works — the nature around us. It seeks sustainable and efficient solutions by emulating nature's time-tested patterns and strategies because 3.8 billion years of evolution means 3.8 billion years of systems and strategies going through iteration after iteration to manage the same operating conditions we're dealing with today. The process and application of applying that wisdom is Biomimicry, but sometimes, we recognize it's best to just use nature itself as a nature-based solution.

Whatever challenge you have, nature has already dealt with it. Don't think so? Let's ask nature.
